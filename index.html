WARNING!

ENTER AT YOUR OWN RISK!

Terror On Route 66 is an intense haunted attraction.

Certain experiences may contain frightening scenes, loud noises, darkness, strobe lighting, fog effects, intense performances, and mature content.

Interactive Touch and Rated R admission options may include additional contact and content. Guests must follow all applicable age restrictions and requirements.

  • Follow all instructions from attraction staff.
  • Do not touch actors or props unless expressly permitted as part of your experience.
  • No running, pushing, or intentionally unsafe behavior.
  • Guests who appear intoxicated or behave dangerously may be refused admission.
  • Interactive and Rated R participants must complete the required waiver.
  • Rated R admission requires guests to be at least 18 years old with valid identification.
