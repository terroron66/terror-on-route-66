VENDORS

BECOME A VENDOR

Thank you for your interest in being a vendor at Terror On Route 66!

We are now accepting vendor applications for our 2026 Season! Participants are encouraged to have Halloween, gothic, oddities, spooky style items but it is NOT required. Vendors are prohibited from selling alcohol, tobacco, products containing cannabis/THC or illegal substances.

Vendor fee is $25 per weekend and a payment link will be sent to the email you provide in this form. Vendor fees are non-refundable. If you are interested in reserving a full season spot, please email us! Your vendor slot will be reserved once payment is received.

Set up is between 5:30-6:30PM. Spaces are limited and first come first serve. Average booth space is 10x10. Vendors may purchase more than one booth space if needed. Vendors are responsible for their own booth setup including tables, chairs, tents and additional lighting. We do not provide electric or water.

FOOD VENDORS: Vendors selling food will have a different vendor fee. Please email us in addition to completing the vendor application to check availability and pricing.
